## Fixed

- **The New Tab bar sits where Dia's does with the address bar in the sidebar.** Without a toolbar above the page, the bar and the logo sat about 20 pt higher than in Dia. They now keep the same place whether the address bar is in the toolbar or the sidebar.
- **Tabs along the top have room to breathe.** The first tab no longer touches the window's green button, and there's as much space under the tabs as above them. The strip that drops down in full screen is evened out too.

## Smaller

- The palette button on the New Tab page sits as far from the bottom edge as from the right.
