## New

- **Ardia is notarized by Apple.** Download it, drag it to Applications and open it: no more System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway on first launch.
- **Developer Tools dock next to the page, like Chrome.** ⌥⌘I or Inspect opens them on the right side of the window. Drag the divider to resize, use Developer Tools' own menu to dock them at the bottom, or undock them into a window and back.
